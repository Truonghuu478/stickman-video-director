# Trends: timing and flavour, not the story

Use trends after you have story seeds, to pick timing or a detail, never as the story itself. A trend answers "why now?", not "what happens?". Both test episodes started from a trend and ended as a topic with no choice or turn.

## Gather (only when no topic is given, or the user asks)

- Endless radar for Vietnam's top TikTok videos of the last 24 h: https://endless.app/radar/tiktok/vietnam (fetch as markdown; displayed counts are totals, not 24-hour gains; brand accounts mixed in).
- 2–3 Vietnamese weekly trend roundups from the last 7 days (Tuổi Trẻ, VTV, Advertising Vietnam).
- Holidays and seasons in the next 14 days (Tết, Noel, mùa thi, 20/11, rét đầu mùa, mưa bão).
- TikTok Creative Center only from links or screenshots the user supplies (crawlers get an empty page).

## Filter

Drop trends that rely on copyrighted music or lyrics, lip-sync, a real person's look or dance, celebrities or KOLs, on-screen text, ads or sponsorships, sensitive news (accidents, violence, scandals), or mockery of looks or groups. Do not copy a specific video.

## Use

- Keep the **behaviour** behind a trend ("ai cũng khoe ảnh cũ để so với bây giờ"), then find a character who wants something inside that behaviour, an obstacle, a choice and a turn ([story-craft](story-craft.md)). If you cannot, drop the trend; an everyday story with no trend is better than a trend with no story.
- Record in `TRENDS.md`: date, links opened, trends kept and dropped with reasons, the behaviour borrowed, and a rough expiry. Pick a publish date before expiry.
- POST.md uses at most one or two real trend hashtags from TRENDS.md.
