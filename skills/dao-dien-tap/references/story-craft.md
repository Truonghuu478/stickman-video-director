# Story craft for 15–30 second silent shorts

A short works when a viewer can retell it in one sentence that contains **a choice** and **a turn**: "Rét quá không muốn dậy, cuối cùng nhường chỗ cho mèo rồi chui vào nằm luôn." A short fails when the retell is a situation: "Một người dọn nhà tìm thấy ảnh cũ."

## The spine

| Time (≈25 s film) | Beat | What the viewer gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | **Hook** | Something already happening, or an odd image. Bright enough and big enough to read on a phone. Optional hook text. |
| 2–6 s | **Want** | What the character wants, shown by a specific behaviour. |
| 6–15 s | **Obstacle, twice** | The world says no; the second "no" is bigger or funnier than the first. |
| on screen, ≥ 1 s | **Choice** | The character does something only this character would do. This is a clip, not a cut. |
| last 3–6 s | **Turn** | The result changes the meaning, or tops what the viewer expected. |
| optional 0.5–1 s | **Button** | One reaction after the turn (a look, the cat, a slump). |

Fewer beats is fine. A missing choice or a missing turn is not.

## Engines that work without dialogue

- **Escalation**: the same problem three times, each worse (rule of three). The third time breaks the pattern.
- **Absurd commitment**: the character solves a small problem with a ridiculous, total solution (brushing teeth inside the quilt).
- **Reversal**: the setup makes us expect A, the character does B, and B is truer to them.
- **Role swap**: the pet, the parent or the object ends up in the character's place.
- **Small sacrifice**: the character gives up something we watched them want. Needs a turn too, or it is only sweet.
- **Gesture instead of words**: the thing they cannot say, done with an object (covering a sleeping parent with a jacket).

## Where stories come from

Specific Vietnamese daily life the audience meets weekly: phòng trọ và bạn cùng phòng, deadline và nước rút lúc 23:59, nhóm chat và "đã xem", sale 0 giờ và ví mỏng cuối tháng, ship đồ ăn, pin điện thoại 1%, xe buýt và mưa, đồ ăn mẹ gửi lên, về quê dịp Tết, thú cưng chiếm nhà, đầu tuần đi làm. These are seeds, not a list to exhaust. A good seed has a **specific behaviour** people recognise ("để báo thức 5 cái cách nhau 5 phút"), not a mood ("lười dậy").

Trends are for timing and flavour, after the story stands on its own ([trends](trends.md)). A trend gives a topic; you still have to find the choice and the turn.

## Five tests (run them on every pitch, honestly)

1. **Mute test.** Describe each beat as one still frame. If a beat needs words to be understood, rewrite the action or give it a ≤ 10-word on-screen line in finishing.
2. **Retell test.** Write the one-sentence retell. It must contain a choice verb and a turn. "Rồi… thì…" is a good sign.
3. **Predictability test.** After beat 3, write the most obvious ending. If your ending is that, change it, or add one more step past it (v1 Chăn ấm ended at "tucks the cat in"; v2 goes one step further: he climbs in too).
4. **Specificity test.** Name the detail a viewer would comment about ("cái kén chăn đánh răng"). "Relatable", "cozy" and "nostalgic" are not details.
5. **Cut test.** Point to the clip where the choice is visible. If the choice falls between two shots, the plan is broken.

## Anti-patterns seen in real episodes

- **State-to-state storytelling**: each shot shows a new situation and the decisions happen off screen. Viewers see results, not a person.
- **Nostalgia with nothing at stake**: finding an old photo and recreating it is a mood, not a story (Thuyền giấy).
- **The first ending**: sweet and expected. Push one step past it.
- **Hook in the dark**: a wide, dim, still first shot with a tiny character. Start on movement, a face, or a strange image.
- **One action stretched over 8 s**: the story may be fine and still feel dead. Size each clip to its action ([shot-plan](shot-plan.md)).
- **Silence**: a silent-actor film still needs a sound bed; room tone alone reads as broken ([finishing](finishing.md)).

## On-screen text

Allowed and often needed, but added in finishing, never generated in the picture:

- **Hook** (0–2.5 s), ≤ 8 words, sets the situation: "POV: Hà Nội trở lạnh lần đầu".
- **Punchline** (last 2–3 s), ≤ 10 words, only when the turn needs a voice: "Thôi, nghỉ 1 bữa chắc không sao đâu".
- Natural Gen Z Vietnamese, no hashtags, no emoji (the bundled font has no emoji).
